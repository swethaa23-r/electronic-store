WE'RE HERE FOR THE WHOLE JOURNEY

From our door to yours.

Everything you need to know about delivery, returns, and where your next everyday favorite is right now.

A smooth arrival.

Our current checkout accepts delivery addresses in the United States. Your delivery progress is available using the private tracking code on your order confirmation.

Shipping costs

Orders of $100 or more ship free. For orders below $100, a $7 delivery fee is shown at checkout.

Processing & delivery

Your order begins with a confirmed status. Once fulfillment is arranged, its progress can be checked on the tracking tab. Delivery dates are not guaranteed at checkout.

Payment on delivery

No payment is taken online. Your order total is due on delivery. We never ask you to enter card details.

Know where it's going.

Save your tracking code after checkout. It is your private key to order status and return requests — don't share it publicly.

Returns, considered.

We want every piece of gear to fit seamlessly into your daily routine. Delivered orders can be submitted for return review within 30 days using your private tracking code.

30-day return eligibility

Hardware must be in clean working condition with its original box, cables, and accessories. Keep all packaging until your return review is confirmed.

Simple return review

Submit your private tracking code and reason using the form on the right. Our team reviews requests within 24 hours and provides a prepaid return label.

Fast replacement or refund

Once your return is inspected at our studio, we issue a full refund or dispatch a brand-new replacement unit at no extra delivery cost.

Delivery questions,
answered.

Our checkout currently accepts delivery addresses across the United States. Every shipment is handled by tracked domestic couriers and includes a private tracking code on your confirmation screen.

Orders begin in a confirmed state and are typically prepared for dispatch within 1–2 business days. Standard transit takes 3–5 business days depending on your state. Live progress is always visible in the Track your order tab.

You only pay when your order arrives at your door. No online card payment is taken at checkout, and your exact order total (including free shipping on orders $100+ or the $7 delivery fee) is shown upfront before you place your order.

Once your order status is marked as delivered, submit a return review request using your private tracking code on the Returns tab. Please keep the original packaging and wait for our confirmation and return label before sending an item back.

Enter your private 6-digit order tracking code into the tracker tool at the top of this page. You will see your exact courier milestone updates—from order confirmation and packaging to dispatch, out-for-delivery, and final doorstep delivery.

If your order is still in the "Confirmed" or "Preparing" state, you can update your delivery destination immediately by contacting our 24/7 support desk with your tracking code. Once an order is dispatched with our courier, rerouting may add 24 hours to transit.
