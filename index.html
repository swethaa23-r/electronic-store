SUPPORT & GUIDANCE

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to help you shop, track orders, and make the most of your hardware.

Placing an order on STACKLY is simple and seamless. Browse through our collections, select your preferred hardware and color option, and click Add to Cart. When you are ready, navigate to your Shopping Cart and proceed to Checkout. Enter your shipping address, select your preferred delivery speed, choose a secure payment method, and confirm your order. You will receive an immediate email confirmation with your order summary and invoice.

We support a broad range of verified payment methods for your convenience: all major Credit and Debit Cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express), Apple Pay, Google Pay, PayPal, UPI payments, and Cash on Delivery (COD) for eligible domestic addresses. Every transaction is encrypted with enterprise-grade 256-bit SSL protocols.

As soon as your package is dispatched from our fulfillment center, we will send an automated dispatch notification with your carrier name and real-time live tracking link via email and SMS. You can also monitor your shipment anytime using the Shipping Returns & Tracking link in our top navigation bar with your Order ID.

We stand behind our curation with a 30-Day Hassle-Free Return Policy. If an item doesn't fit your workflow or expectations, you can request a return within 30 days of delivery. Items must be returned in their original packaging with all included cables and accessories. Once inspected, full refunds are issued to your original payment method within 3–5 business days.

Yes. All hardware purchased on STACKLY is 100% brand new, authentic, and backed by a comprehensive 2-Year Official Manufacturer Warranty. This coverage protects against manufacturing defects, internal component failures, and hardware irregularities. Verified warranty service and repair support are facilitated directly through our customer care desk.

Our dedicated support specialists are ready to help. You can email us anytime at support@stacklytech.com, open a ticket via our Help Center, or use our quick assistance modal. Support inquiries are typically answered within 1–2 hours during operational hours (Mon–Sat, 9 AM – 8 PM EST).
